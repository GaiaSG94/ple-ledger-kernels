The model never reads the drawing

github.com/GaiaSG94/ple-ledger-kernels · commit ········ (SHA 40 car. au commit final) · CC BY 4.0
Main finding. Taking reading and arithmetic away from the model raises the dev-set score from 0.11 to 0.31 while tokens per task fall from 62 k to 24 k. The cost moves from tokens to CPU.

1. Why reading and arithmetic break agents

On the dev set, the two heaviest failure families are not reasoning errors: they are misread drawing callouts and unit errors. A callout like 6000 means millimetres; read as metres, the moment is off by a factor of a million. Models are good at deciding what to compute and poor at small print and units, so the model only writes a typed spec and code does the rest.
