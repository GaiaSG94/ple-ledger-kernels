§1 · for readers without an engineering background

01 · the problemWhy reading and arithmetic break agents

An engineering task hands the agent a drawing, a specification and a question: how much does this beam bend, which cable section carries this load. On the dev set, the two heaviest failure families are not reasoning errors. They are misread drawing callouts and unit errors.

A callout like 6000 on a structural drawing means millimetres. Read as metres, the bending moment is off by a factor of a million, and the model writes it down calmly. Language models are good at deciding what to compute and poor at small print and units. So we split the job.

04 · limitsWhere it still fails

  • Twelve kernels, not more. Other tasks take the generic sympy/pint path, slower and lower-scoring.
  • No paid code tables. Normative values come from the task's own tables or are recomputed, source cited.
  • Raster drawings depend on OCR confidence; the verbatim text always travels with the ledger.
  • Replicating public verify scripts was dropped: it reads as hard-coded output.
