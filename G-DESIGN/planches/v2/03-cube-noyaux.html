Main finding

Taking reading and arithmetic away from the model raises the dev-set score from 0.11 to 0.31 while tokens per task fall from 62 k to 24 k. The cost moves from tokens to CPU.

§1 · for ML readers

01 · the problemWhy reading and arithmetic break agents

On the dev set, the two heaviest failure families are not reasoning errors. They are misread drawing callouts and unit errors. A callout like 6000 means millimetres; read as metres, the moment is off by a factor of a million, and the model writes it down calmly.

Models are good at deciding what to compute and poor at small print and units. So the model only writes a typed spec; extraction, arithmetic and checking are code.

04 · limitsWhere it still fails

  • Twelve kernels, not more. Other tasks take the generic sympy/pint path, slower and lower-scoring.
  • No paid code tables. Normative values come from the task's own tables or are recomputed, source cited.
  • Raster drawings depend on OCR confidence; the verbatim text always travels with the ledger.
  • Replicating public verify scripts was dropped: it reads as hard-coded output.
